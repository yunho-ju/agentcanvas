# Show where each fact came from

A reader should be able to check any fact you state without asking you where
it came from.

## How to do it

1. Put the source next to the fact, not in a list at the end.
2. Name the document and the place inside it: title, section or page.
3. Quote the sentence you relied on when the wording matters.
4. If a tool or search gave you the fact, say which one and when.
5. If you cannot find a source, say so plainly: "I could not find a source for
   this." Do not fill the gap with a guess.

## What good looks like

> The deadline is 31 March (Handbook 2026, section 4.2: "applications close on
> 31 March").

## What to avoid

- A link with no hint of what is inside it.
- A source that does not actually say what you claim it says.
- Presenting your own inference as something a source stated.

When two sources disagree, show both and say which one you followed and why.
