# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The AgentCanvas community is committed to a respectful environment for participants regardless of background, identity or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Respect other participants' time, perspectives and boundaries.
- Direct technical criticism at code, behavior and evidence rather than people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and respond to questions and feedback constructively.
- Report security, privacy and licensing problems responsibly.
- Acknowledge impact and help repair harm after mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination, hate speech or unwanted sexual attention
- disclosure of personal information, credentials or private conversations without consent
- repeated insults, deliberate disruption, stalking or unwanted contact
- exploitation or premature disclosure of vulnerabilities
- impersonating the project or falsely claiming to represent it

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and official project spaces, and when someone publicly represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For non-sensitive public-thread problems, maintainers may request a behavior change, hide or remove content, or lock a thread. For reports involving personal information, safety or retaliation risk, use the private reporting procedure in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and include `Conduct report` in the title. Do not open a public issue.

Depending on severity, maintainers may issue a warning, remove content, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban a participant. Reporter identity and report details are shared only as needed for review. A maintainer with a conflict of interest must recuse.

## Attribution

This policy adapts principles from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) to the current small open-source project.
